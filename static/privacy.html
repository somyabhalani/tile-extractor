Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Processing

The Tile Extractor tool processes PDF catalogues to extract product images. All processing is performed either locally on your machine or on our secure transient servers depending on your version. Uploaded files are deleted automatically after processing.

2. No Data Retention

We do not store your PDF files, extracted images, or any catalogue data longer than required for the active extraction session. Once you close your session, the data is purged from our temporary storage.

3. AI Processing

Text extraction is assisted by NVIDIA Vision AI. During the "Scan Text" phase, base64-encoded images of the catalogue pages are sent to NVIDIA's inference API. NVIDIA does not use this data for model training during transient inference sessions.
